# App flow

An opening invitation greets Ma. Open your surprise starts the birthday melody from that tap and slides the invitation away. Open without music enters quietly. Reduced motion skips the slide. A failed audio request never blocks entry, and no-script or missing-copy visits skip the invitation entirely.

The greeting addresses Ma and names Alma Merong. Five candles can be tapped separately or extinguished together. Completing the wish creates a short celebration; Make another wish resets all five candles.

The garden below contains five flower buttons. Each shows a different birthday wish and updates a discovery count. Repeated selections do not increase the count.

The letter opens and closes inline, showing the message John supplied and the signature John Andrei. Long messages remain in the document flow.

Play music starts a short birthday melody. Stop music ends it. Hiding the page also stops it; returning does not restart it. Unsupported playback shows Music unavailable. Motion can be paused separately, and reduced motion is respected automatically.

The closing section offers four PNG card downloads. Scroll gathers and spreads each pair of cards; pause and reduced motion keep them in their static readable pose. Share opens the device share sheet when available, otherwise copies the public link. Clipboard denial reveals a selectable link. Cancelling the share sheet leaves the page quiet.

Without JavaScript, the greeting, card download and native letter remain available; a note explains the candles and lists the flower wishes. No login, permission prompt or remote data is required.

The language button switches English and Filipino while keeping candles, flower selection and letter state. A Filipino share link includes ?lang=fil. If the translation file fails, the page keeps its native letter and card and explains that refreshing may restore the interactive surprises.
